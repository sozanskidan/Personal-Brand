Personal Design System

Dan Sozanski

Apple-esque restraint, gallery-quiet, professional casual. A system for documents, slides, and sheets.

Reading something built in this system should leave the audience feeling relieved, informed, motivated.

Principles

01

Whitespace is the brand

When in doubt, remove. Density signals panic.

02

One accent per view

The grey-blue accent is a scalpel, not a highlighter.

03

Type does the design

Davinci headings against SF Pro body is the entire mood. Don't decorate it.

Surface Conventions

Same tokens, three contexts. The personality stays constant.

Google Docs

Title in display serif. TL;DR callout at top. Sections in h2. One accent moment per doc.

Google Slides

16:9 only. Minimal text. Title-only and image-only slides. No transitions, cuts only.

Google Sheets

Minimal gridlines. Label-caps headers. Numbers right-aligned. One accent highlight per sheet.

Do's & Don'ts

Do

Lead every document with a TL;DR.
Use one accent moment per view. Make it the most important thing.
Default to whitespace. Strip until it feels too sparse, then add one thing back.
Let Davinci (or Instrument Serif) headlines carry the emotional weight.
Right-align numbers. Always.

Don't

Use em-dashes. Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead.
Mix more than two type families on a single surface.
Use the accent more than once per slide or section.
Add drop shadows, gradients, or glows.
Use banded rows in tables.
Bold a heading. Size and serif are enough.
Center-align body copy.
