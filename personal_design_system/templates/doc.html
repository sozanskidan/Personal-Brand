Spatial Vignettes: Week of June 1

Progress update on the vignette system, user research findings, and next steps for the prototype sprint.

TL;DR

Three of five vignettes are in high-fidelity prototype. User research surfaced a strong preference for the "arrival moment" transition (4.2/5 desirability). Remaining two vignettes are blocked on spatial audio API access. Sprint closes June 6.

Prototype progress

The vignette system now covers three core scenarios: morning context (bedroom to kitchen), commute handoff (phone to headset), and workspace entry (hallway to desk). Each vignette runs 8 to 12 seconds and transitions between spatial zones using a shared easing curve.

The arrival moment, where the environment audio fades in before the visual scene resolves, tested as the highest-rated interaction in the desirability study. Participants described it as "calming" and "intentional." We are treating this as the signature transition for the system.

Research findings

We ran five moderated sessions with internal participants (mixed roles: engineering, design, PM). The protocol asked participants to experience each vignette, then rate desirability on a 5-point scale and describe the transition in their own words.

Two patterns emerged. First, audio-first transitions were consistently preferred over visual-first ones. Participants said the sound "prepared" them for the space. Second, shorter vignettes (under 10 seconds) scored higher than longer ones, regardless of content complexity. People want to arrive, not watch a loading screen with better aesthetics.

One participant noted: "It felt like walking into a room where someone already turned the lights on for you." That framing resonated with the team and we are using it as the internal design principle for the remaining two vignettes.

Next steps

The remaining two vignettes (focus session, wind-down) require the spatial audio positioning API, which is in development by the platform team. We have a dependency on their June 3 milestone. If that lands on time, we can hit our June 6 sprint close.

In parallel, we are refining the arrival moment curve and documenting the transition spec for the engineering handoff. The goal is a single, reusable transition component that any vignette can call.
