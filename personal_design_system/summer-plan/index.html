01 · The problem

The loop, and my part in it

The back pain is the trigger, but the real engine is follow-through and trust. Naming the loop honestly is the first step to running it the other way.

02 · The solution

What actually gets us out of it

The heart of it

Make a real plan, then keep it. Be consistent and steady about the upkeep so the trust has something solid to stand on. The point is not a perfect back or a grand gesture. It is becoming someone whose word can be relied on, starting with the word I give myself.

Treat the back as a daily practice, not a project with a finish line.

Work with Eric on a version gentle enough to do every single day, and scale it down on rough days instead of stopping. Stopping is what resets everything to zero, and the restart is the real enemy, not the soreness.

Take the mind-body link seriously, because for me it is real.

When I am calm and present, my back is better and so are we. The anxiety and the back are the same system. The nervous-system work and therapy are not side projects, they are how I show up steadier for Nicole.

Get better at the hard conversations.

When Nicole has difficult feelings, I want to stay in it with her instead of getting overwhelmed and shutting down. Communicating openly, especially when it is uncomfortable, is something I am choosing to work on, not avoid. Therapy is the lever here.

Schedule the joy, do not wait to feel like it.

Biking, photography, golf, travel, flirting. None of it happens on motivation alone, so it goes on the calendar like everything else. Joy is part of the fix, not the reward for finishing it.

To Nicole

I am not asking you to believe a transformed version of me. I am asking you to watch me keep small promises, starting now, and to build this with me: the spirituality, the dates, being healthy for the family we want, goals we set together with a real plan and a way to hold each other to it.

There have been plenty of moments, Spain among them, that showed us both what we are like when I am calm and present. I want to get back to that on purpose, not by accident. A steady routine, a healthier life, a future we are building together, in work and in life, a home and a family. I am not going to give up, and I want to lean into the hard parts with you instead of away from them.

I love you, Nicole.
